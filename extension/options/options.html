Reference lookups

Clicking a citation looks that one reference up — nothing is sent unless you click, and never for a whole bibliography at once. The card names the service that answered. Results are kept on this computer so reopening a paper costs no further lookups (matches for 30 days, misses for 7); nothing is synced anywhere.
